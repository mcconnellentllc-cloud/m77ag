2. Engine Oil and Oil Filter

The oil fill cap on this engine reads "SAE 0W-20 dexos." That is the requirement, not a suggestion. Oil that does not meet dexos1 can void warranty coverage on engine damage.

PartNumberNotes
Oil filter (OE) ACDelco PF63E
GM 19330000		 The number listed in the 2017 owner manual replacement parts table. Both O'Reilly and AutoZone stock the ACDelco line.
Oil filter (older OE number) ACDelco PF63
GM 89017525		 Superseded by PF63E. Same filter, same fitment. If the counter shows PF63, it is correct.
Engine oil SAE 0W-20 Must carry the dexos1 approval mark. 8.0 quarts with filter change, so two 5-quart jugs or two gallons.

On aftermarket cross-references. Published crosses for the PF63 are inconsistent between catalogs, and several common substitutes use a lower bypass valve setting than the GM filter. Commonly listed equivalents are Wix 57502, NAPA Gold 7502, Purolator L22500, Fram PH10575, MicroGard MGL10575 (O'Reilly house brand), and STP S10590. Buy the ACDelco unless the counter can confirm the substitute by VIN.

Change interval

Change the oil and filter when the CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON message appears, within the next 600 miles, or at least once a year regardless of mileage. Reset the oil life system after every change. Before a long trip, change it regardless of remaining oil life percentage if the vehicle is anywhere near due.

3. Engine Air Filter

Read this section before buying. The printed owner manual gives the wrong part number for this vehicle.

PartNumberNotes
Engine air filter (correct) ACDelco A3218C
GM 23349854		 GM parts catalog fitment is 2017-2020 Escalade and Escalade ESV. This is the one to buy.
Listed cross-reference Wix 42488 Published as a cross for GM 23349854. Confirm at the counter by VIN before buying a substitute.

Do not buy ACDelco A3181C / GM 22845992. The 2017 Escalade owner manual replacement parts table lists that number, but the manual was printed 7/6/16 and the number is wrong for this model year. GM's parts catalog shows 22845992 fitting 2002-2016 Escalade only.

If a counter system pulls up A3181C for this VIN, stop and have them look up GM 23349854 directly.

Change interval

GM's schedule calls for replacement every 45,000 miles or four years, whichever comes first, on both the normal and severe schedules. The manual adds: if driving in dusty conditions, inspect at every oil change or more often. This vehicle runs farm roads, so treat the four-year clock as the governing one.

4. Fuel Filter

There is nothing to buy. This is the correct answer, not a gap in the research.

The 2017 Escalade has no serviceable fuel filter. The filter is built into the fuel pump module inside the fuel tank and is not sold or replaced separately. The phrase "fuel filter" does not appear once in the 397-page factory owner manual, and no fuel filter replacement appears anywhere in the maintenance schedule.

What the manual does require for the fuel system, at each 7,500-mile service:

  • Visually inspect the fuel system for damage or leaks.
  • Inspect the evaporative control system at 30,000-mile intervals, checking all fuel and vapor lines and hoses for proper attachment, routing, and condition.

If a parts store offers to sell you a fuel filter for this VIN, it is either the wrong application or a complete in-tank fuel pump module assembly running several hundred dollars. Neither is routine maintenance. Decline it. A pump module is replaced only when the pump fails or fuel delivery is diagnosed as restricted.

5. Passenger Compartment Air Filter

Not one of the three asked for, but it is the shortest interval on the schedule and the cheapest comfort item on a long drive.

PartNumberNotes
Cabin air filter ACDelco CF188
GM 23281440		 Listed in the 2017 owner manual replacement parts table. Behind the glove box.

Interval is every 22,500 miles or two years, whichever comes first. Replace sooner with reduced airflow, window fogging, or odors.

Wiper blades

Both front blades are the same length on this vehicle, which is unusual and easy to get wrong at the counter. The manual lists one part number for driver and passenger alike.

PositionSizePart
Driver front 55 cm / 21.7 in. (buy 22 in.) GM 22756331
Rain-X Latitude 5079279
Passenger front 55 cm / 21.7 in. (buy 22 in.) GM 22756331
Rain-X Latitude 5079279
Rear 33 cm / 13.0 in. GM 22956295
Rain-X rear, by application

Rain-X part number 5079279 is the 22 in. size in both blade lines. The suffix names the line: 5079279-1 is standard Latitude, 5079279-2 is Latitude Water Repellency, which transfers a Rain-X coating onto the glass. O'Reilly also stocks the 22 in. Latitude under its own number 810109.

The rear blade is not a universal part. Rear arms use vehicle-specific attachments, so have the counter look the rear blade up by year, make and model rather than asking for a 13 in. blade off the shelf.

Also listed in the manual for reference: spark plugs are GM 12622441 / ACDelco 41-114, gap 0.037-0.043 in. Transfer case takes DEXRON-VI, 1.6 qt. Rear axle takes SAE 75W-85 synthetic; front axle takes SAE 75W-90 synthetic.
