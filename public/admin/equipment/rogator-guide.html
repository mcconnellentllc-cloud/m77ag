Where the settings are

  • Steering: ISOBUS VT → AutoTrac → AutoTrac Settings. The Steer Sensitivity and Acquire Sensitivity bars, plus the Restore Defaults and Advanced Settings buttons. The Setup and Diagnostics buttons are on the right side.
  • Receiver: ISOBUS VT → StarFire 7000 → Main. Tabs are Info, Setup, License Type and Serial Port.

Position mode is SF1, not RTK. SF1 is a basic correction level, accurate to several inches from pass to pass, and it drifts over time. It does not cause the S-curves described in Section 2, but it limits how straight and repeatable any pass can be. Check the License Type tab for an active RTK license, and check that the RTK radio is receiving the base station. Fix those before judging guidance accuracy.

2. Problem: Weaving Instead of a Straight Line

On guidance the RoGator drives a smooth, repeating S-curve instead of a straight line. The tracks swing several feet each side of the line and cross the passes next to them.

A regular S-curve is an oscillation, not a GPS problem. The steering corrects too late, overshoots the line, then corrects back the other way.

On a sprayer with hydrostatic steering, the usual causes are, in order: slack between the ATU motor and the steering, wrong machine measurements, a bad steering calibration, and sensitivity set wrong for that slack. Work through Section 3 before changing sensitivity. Tuning sensitivity will not fix a mechanical or measurement problem.

Read the pattern

What the machine doesWhat it points to
Crosses the line again and again in a regular S-curveOvershoot. There is lag in the steering, or sensitivity is too high for the slack.
Drifts slowly to one side and stays off the lineSensitivity too low, or the ATU motor is slipping on the wheel.
Short, fast jerks of the wheelSensitivity too high.
Good one direction, off-line the other directionReceiver offsets or terrain compensation calibration (Section 3).
Weaves only on a side slopeTerrain compensation calibration or receiver height.

3. Checks Before Tuning

Do these in order. Each one can cause weaving on its own, and no sensitivity setting will make up for them.

1. Only one steering system active

  • The Raven Viper Pro still shows SmarTrax. Confirm SmarTrax is disengaged and cannot steer while the ATU is driving.
  • If the SmarTrax hydraulic steering valve is still plumbed into the steering circuit, confirm it is not leaking oil past the orbital. Two systems acting on the same steering will fight and weave.

2. Mechanical slack

  • ATU motor mounting: tight to the steering column, with the anti-rotation bracket firm and not flexing.
  • Motor to steering wheel: no slipping. Mark the wheel and the motor, run a pass, and check that the marks still line up.
  • Steering free play: with the engine running, measure how far the wheel turns before the tires move. Excess play in the orbital, the cylinders or the tie rods shows up as overshoot.
  • Tire pressures equal side to side. Front end alignment and toe within spec.

3. Machine measurements

On the Setup screen, check the machine type is a self-propelled sprayer. Measure and enter:

  • Wheelbase: front axle center to rear axle center.
  • Receiver fore/aft: the receiver's distance from the rear axle (or whatever reference point the screen asks for).
  • Receiver height: ground to the top of the receiver.
  • Receiver left/right offset: 0 if it is centered on the cab.

Wrong wheelbase or receiver fore/aft is a common cause of a regular S-curve. The controller steers for a machine that turns differently from the real one. Use a tape measure; don't estimate.

4. Terrain compensation (TCM) calibration

  1. Park on level, firm ground.
  2. Run the calibration from the StarFire 7000 Setup tab.
  3. When it asks, turn 180° and park in the same tire tracks. Then complete the calibration.

A pitch reading of 3.5° while parked is fine on a slope. On level ground, pitch and roll should both read close to 0° after calibration.

5. ATU steering calibration

Run the steering calibration from the AutoTrac Setup screen in an open, level area at the speed the screen asks for. Run it again after any change to the motor mounting or the steering hydraulics.

4. Dialing In Steer Sensitivity

Only after Section 3 is complete. Steer Sensitivity sets how hard the system turns the wheel to get back to the line. Acquire Sensitivity only affects how quickly it pulls onto a new line.

Procedure

  1. Pick a straight AB line on level ground, at least 1,000 ft long. Run it at normal spraying speed.
  2. Engage AutoTrac and let the machine settle on the line for about 15 seconds.
  3. Watch the 95% Lateral Error and 95% Heading Error for the Current Pass on the AutoTrac Settings screen. Record both values in the log at the end of the pass.
  4. Change Steer Sensitivity by 5 at a time:
    • Overshoots and crosses the line in a regular S-curve: lower it by 5.
    • Drifts off the line and is slow to come back: raise it by 5.
  5. Run the same line again at the same speed. Keep the setting that gives the lowest 95% lateral error with no visible weave.
  6. Change one setting at a time. Leave Acquire Sensitivity at 95 until the straight-line steering is right.

Targets

ReadingTarget on SF1
95% lateral errorUnder 4 in on a straight line, with no visible S-curve in the tracks
95% heading errorUnder 1.0°
Steering wheelSmall, smooth corrections. No hunting back and forth.

If the weave stays the same at every sensitivity setting, the problem is in Section 3: slack, a slipping motor, wrong measurements, or a second steering system. Use Restore Defaults to get back to a known starting point, and send a photo of the Advanced Settings screen before changing anything there.
