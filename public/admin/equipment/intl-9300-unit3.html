The valve cover carries an electrical shock warning for a 90 to 110 volt unit injector system. That is the electronic unit injector system of the 3406E, so this engine is the electronic 3406E. Service it with Cat electronic service tools (Cat ET) and the 3406E parts book.

The engine serial number is on the information label (torn here) and in the engine ECM. Read it with Cat ET or a heavy-duty scan tool; the Cat dealer needs it for exact parts.

Trailer ABS power

This truck was built August 25, 1995. Trucks built before March 1, 1997 were not required to supply constant power to the 7-way center pin, which trailer ABS needs whenever the key is on. Before pulling an ABS trailer such as Unit #106, put a meter on the center pin (pin 7, blue) with the key on and the lights off. No voltage means the center pin needs to be wired to key-on power.
